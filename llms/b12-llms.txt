## Plans

B12 offers two plan families. **DIY plans** are self-serve subscriptions
where the customer builds and manages their own site with B12's AI tools.
**Expert plans** include hands-on work from B12's design, content, and
launch team in addition to the platform.

### DIY Plans

- **Free** — Get online quickly on a B12-branded subdomain. Includes mobile responsive website, AI website generation, and the visual editor.
- **Website** — Launch a polished website on a custom domain. Adds advanced team management, analytics, integrations, and ad removal.
- **Website + Client Engagement** — Everything in Website, plus the tools to run a service business online: custom email address, online payments and invoicing, client intake, email marketing, contact manager, contracts and eSignatures, and file uploads.

### Expert Plans

- **Professional** — B12's team handles website setup and ongoing updates. Includes all client engagement features, business directory listings, and phone support.
- **Advanced** — Designed for businesses with teams that need more seats, more coordination, and priority support.

## Commerce

- Plans are billed in USD, with monthly or annual billing intervals.
- AI credit allowances, seat counts, and current promotional offers vary by plan and may change over time.
- For current pricing, plan comparisons, and the most up-to-date feature list, see https://www.b12.io/pricing/.
